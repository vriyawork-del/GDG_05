About the community

GDG on Campus MMMUT is a student-run group supported by Google Developers. We turn curiosity about technology into skills, projects and friendships.

Our mission: make learning to build software friendly, practical and open to every branch and year.

Our vision: a campus where every student ships something they are proud of.

Questions, answered

Who can join?

Any MMMUT student from any branch or year. You do not need to know how to code.

Is it free?

Yes. Membership and all our workshops are free.

How much time does it take?

As little as one event a month. Do more when you want to.
